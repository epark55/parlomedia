Reminds you until it’s done.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: support@parlomedia.com. I reply within a few days.

I’m not getting reminders.

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, then Stickler, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. In Stickler, open Settings and send yourself a test notification. You can also choose the time of day reminders arrive there.

When do reminders start and stop?

Each reminder starts a set number of days before it’s due, which you choose when you add it. Once it’s due, Stickler reminds you every day until you check it off. Checking it off moves it to the next due date.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search “Stickler”, and pick a size. Lock Screen widgets are added the same way from the Lock Screen.

How do I move my list to a new phone?

In Stickler, open Settings and use Export under Your list. Send that file to your new phone, then use Import in the same place.

What does the unlock include?

The free version holds 3 reminders with every feature. A one-time purchase adds unlimited reminders. There’s no subscription, and it’s shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the unlock screen in Stickler and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What should I put in a note?

Short reminders, like a filter size or “autopay on”. Don’t save passwords, PINs, or account or card numbers: notes can appear in notifications on your lock screen, and in exports and printouts.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No account and no internet. Your reminders and history are stored only on your iPhone.

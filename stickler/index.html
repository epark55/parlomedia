Stickler

Repeating reminders that keep nudging you until the job is actually done.

Free · Unlock

Stickler is for the things that come around every month and are easy to let slip: bills, filters, refills, check-ins. Add a task with a due date, and Stickler reminds you before it's due, then every day after until you check it off.

  • Reminds until doneReminders start before the due date and keep coming daily once it's past, until you check the task off.
  • Repeats on your scheduleMonthly by default, or pick your own repeat. Checking a task off sets up the next one.
  • Notes and amountsAdd a description and an amount or note to each task, like a bill total or account number hint.
  • Home Screen widgetSee what's due next without opening the app.
  • Export and importSave your list to a file and bring it back on a new phone.
  • Light and Dark ModeClean and light by default; follows your iPhone when it's in Dark Mode.

Support

I'm not getting reminders.

Open Settings › Notifications › Stickler on your iPhone and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Focus modes can also hold notifications back, so check Settings › Focus too.

How do I stop the reminders for a task?

Check the task off. Stickler stops reminding you and, for repeating tasks, schedules the next due date.

How do I change how often a task repeats?

Open the task and change its repeat setting. Monthly is the default.

How do I move my tasks to a new phone?

Use Export in Stickler to save your list as a file, then Import it in Stickler on the new phone.

I bought the unlock. How do I restore it?

Use Restore Purchases in Stickler while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Still need help? Email support@parlomedia.com with your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and we'll get back to you.

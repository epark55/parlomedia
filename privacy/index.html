Privacy Policy

For Stickler, Parlo Moon and Pup Studio.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Parlo Media LLC ("Parlo Media", "we") makes the apps Stickler, Parlo Moon and Pup Studio. We built them to work without collecting your personal information. This policy explains what each app uses and where that information goes.

SticklerParlo MoonPup Studio

The short version

  • We don't collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • Our apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you put into our apps stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

Stickler

  • Your tasks, due dates, notes and amounts are stored on your device. They are not sent to Parlo Media or anyone else.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your device by iOS.
  • If you export your list, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Parlo Media never receives it.
  • If you buy the unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Parlo Moon

  • Parlo Moon uses your location to calculate moon times for where you are. Your location is used on your device and is not sent to Parlo Media.
  • To show the name of your area, approximate coordinates are sent to Apple's location service. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • For the cloud forecast, approximate coordinates are sent to the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov). No name, account or device identifier is sent with it.
  • Your purchase is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Pup Studio

  • Pup Studio only uses the photos you choose. Your photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to Parlo Media or anyone else.
  • Finished portraits are saved to your photo library or shared only when you choose to.
  • If you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Purchases

All purchases, including paid apps and in-app unlocks, are processed by Apple. Apple tells us about sales in summary form. We never receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Crash reports and diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and what you send only to answer you. We don't add you to any mailing list or share your message.

Children

Our apps don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how an app handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

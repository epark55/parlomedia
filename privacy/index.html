Privacy Policy

Parlo Media LLC · Effective November 27, 2026

This policy covers the apps published by Parlo Media LLC. Each app has its own section below, and a section is added here before a new app is released. In short: our apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and Parlo Media does not collect or receive your personal information.

Parlo Moon

What we collect. Nothing. Parlo Media does not collect, store, sell or share any data from the app, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Location. If you tap "Use my location", the app reads your location on your iPhone to work out moonrise and moonset. The location, and any coordinates you type in, are saved only on your device. The app's widget, on the same device, reads the same saved location. It is never sent to Parlo Media.

Services the app contacts. A few features need the internet, and the app sends only what each one needs:

  • Place names. To show a place name for your location, the app asks Apple's location services, which are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud cover (United States only). To show a cloud forecast, the app sends your approximate coordinates to the National Weather Service (weather.gov), which returns the forecast. Like any website, the service sees your device's IP address.

Notifications. Moonrise and moonset reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Printing and sharing. Calendars and images you save, print or share are created on your iPhone and go only where you send them.

Children. The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Stickler

What we collect. Nothing. Parlo Media does not collect, store, sell or share any data from the app, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

Your reminders. Reminders, amounts, notes and your completion history are saved only on your iPhone. The app’s widgets, on the same device, read the same saved list. None of it is sent to Parlo Media.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Exporting, printing and sharing. Lists, checklists and history you export, print, copy or share are created on your iPhone and go only where you send them.

Purchases. The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple. Parlo Media never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children. The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Pup Studio

What we collect. Nothing. Parlo Media does not collect, store, sell or share any data from the app, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your photos. Photos you choose or take are processed on your iPhone or iPad, including finding and cutting out your dog. They are never uploaded or sent to Parlo Media. The app asks for permission to use the camera only if you take a photo, and to add to your photo library only when you save a portrait.

Projects. Your last edit is saved on your device. Project files you save with Save Project are stored where you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive, and go only where you send them.

Sharing and printing. Portraits and cards you save, share or print are created on your device and go only where you send them.

Purchases. Pup Studio Pro is sold and processed by Apple. Parlo Media never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. The app keeps a count of free watermark-free saves on your device.

Children. The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: support@parlomedia.com.

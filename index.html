About Parlo Media

Parlo Media is a photo, video and audio business serving St. Clairsville, Ohio and the surrounding area. We help families save the memories stored on aging tapes, slides and prints, and help people and businesses look their best with professional headshots and portraits.

You work directly with us from your first message to final delivery. Scheduling is flexible, so it's easy to fit a session around your calendar.

Proud PPA member. The Professional Photographers of America is the world's largest nonprofit trade association for professional photographers, and a great resource for knowing what to look for when you choose a photographer.

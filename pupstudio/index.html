Pup Studio

Turn everyday photos of your dog into studio portraits.

Free · Pro unlock

Pup Studio lifts your dog out of an ordinary photo and puts them in a studio: pick the lighting, pick the backdrop, place them just right, and save a portrait that looks like it came from a pro shoot.

  • Automatic cut-outPup Studio separates your dog from the background. Add to or trim the cut-out with a brush when you need to.
  • Studio lightingChoose from studio lighting looks to give your portrait shape and depth.
  • BackdropsPick a colored studio backdrop or use one of your own photos as the background.
  • Place and poseDrag your dog anywhere in the frame and size them to fit.
  • The whole packPut several dogs in one portrait: add each one, approve or retake, then arrange them together.
  • iPhone and iPadStart a portrait on your iPhone and finish it on your iPad.

Support

What does Pro include?

Pro removes the Pup Studio watermark from your saved portraits. Everyone gets 3 free Pro saves to try it, and the one-time Pro unlock makes every save watermark-free.

The cut-out missed part of my dog.

Use the add and erase brushes to fix the edge. Photos with good light and a dog that stands out from the background cut out best.

Pup Studio can't see my photos.

Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Pup Studio and choose which photos it can use.

I bought Pro. How do I restore it?

Use Restore Purchases in Pup Studio while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Still need help? Email support@parlomedia.com with your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and we'll get back to you.

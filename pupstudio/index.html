Pup Studio

Studio portraits of your dog, from an everyday photo.

Coming soon to the App Store · Free, with a one-time Pro unlock

Nothing beats a professional photographer’s portrait of your dog, but Pup Studio comes close. Pick a photo, and the app cuts your dog out, adds studio lighting and a backdrop, and gets it ready to share, print or send as a card. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: support@parlomedia.com. We usually reply within a few days.

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time purchase. It removes the watermark from every saved photo and lets you use Save Project to keep editing later or on another device. Without Pro you can still save photos with a small Pup Studio mark, and you get 3 watermark-free saves to try it out.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Tap Get Pro on the home screen, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

The cutout missed part of my dog, or kept some background.

Open the Cutout tab. Paint with Add to bring back fur, or with Erase to remove background. Pinch to zoom in for fine edges. A photo with the dog in front of a plain, lighter background cuts out most cleanly.

My dog looks purple, blue or green.

Colored room lights, such as LED strips, can tint fur. On the Light tab, turn on Fix colored room light.

How do I make a portrait of two or more dogs?

On the home screen, tap Group Photo (2+ dogs) and choose a photo of each dog. Then use Arrange for Me on the Frame tab to try a few layouts. You can still drag and resize each dog afterwards.

How do I make a card or print?

On the Frame tab, pick a size under Cards & prints, such as 4×6, 5×7 or 4¼×5½, or set a custom size. Rotate Frame switches between tall and wide. Then tap Save Photo and print it or send it to a print service.

How do I pick up where I left off?

Tap Resume Last Edit on the home screen. With Pro, Save Project also saves a project file you can open later, or on your iPad, with Open a Project.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No. There is no account, and your photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded. The only thing that goes online is the Pro purchase, which Apple handles.
